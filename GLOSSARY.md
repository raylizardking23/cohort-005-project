# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary pins down the language for getting money back on a purchase, so the screens, emails and code all say the same thing.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and watches courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: User, Customer, Student

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who resolves Refund Requests.

### Buying a course

**Personal Purchase**:
A learner buying a course for themselves.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Seat Purchase**:
A learner buying one or more seats on a course for their team, each handed out as a coupon.
_Avoid_: Team licence, Bulk purchase

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. It ends when their Refund Request is Approved, and a new Personal Purchase starts a new one.
_Avoid_: Access, Subscription, Membership

**Progress**:
How far a learner has got in a course: lessons completed out of the total, and time watched. It outlives an Enrollment, so a learner who buys again picks up where they left off.
_Avoid_: Completion, Usage

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have the money for a Personal Purchase returned to them, along with their reason. It is a request we decide on, not a guarantee, and a Personal Purchase can have only one. A Seat Purchase cannot have one.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The return of the full Refund Amount to the learner, which follows an Approved Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
What the learner actually paid for the purchase, after any discount, coupon or purchasing-power price.
_Avoid_: List price, Refundable amount

**Refund Eligibility**:
The rules a purchase must meet before a Refund Request can be made: within the Refund Window, under the watch-time and lesson-completion limits, and not a claimed team seat.

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Resolution**:
An Admin's decision to Approve or Decline a Refund Request. It is final and cannot be undone.
_Avoid_: Review, Outcome

### Refund Request statuses

**Open**:
The Refund Request has been made and is waiting for a Resolution.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Raised

**Approved**:
An Admin has agreed the learner gets their money back. The learner loses access to the course at that moment and their rating is removed, but their Progress and comments are kept. A Refund starts.

**Declined**:
An Admin has turned the Refund Request down, with a Decline Note, so no money is returned. This is final for that purchase.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Decline Note**:
The Admin's explanation to the learner of why their Refund Request was Declined.
_Avoid_: Rejection reason, Comment

### Refund statuses

**Pending**:
The Refund has been approved but the money has not been sent yet.

**Issued**:
The money has been sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed**:
We tried to send the money back and it did not go through. An Admin retries it, which makes it Pending again.

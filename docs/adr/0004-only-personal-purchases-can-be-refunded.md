# Only Personal Purchases can be refunded

A Refund Request can only be made on a Personal Purchase, never on a Seat Purchase. Both are recorded as purchases against the buyer, and the refund rules used to look at the buyer's latest purchase of a course, so someone who bought the course and then bought seats would have been refunding the seats. Refunding seats raises questions this work does not answer: what happens to unclaimed coupons, to seats already handed out, and to a partial refund for some seats. Excluding them is the deliberate "no" until those are decided.

## Consequences

- The learner who bought seats cannot get money back for them through the app. That has to happen outside it until Seat Purchase refunds are designed.
